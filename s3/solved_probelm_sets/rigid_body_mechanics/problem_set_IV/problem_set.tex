\section{(Homework)}

Let the solid S be a homogeneous disk, of negligible section, of mass m, of center G and radius a (figure 3). We propose to study the motion of the solid S with respect to the fixed reference frame $R_o(O, \vec{x}_o, \vec{y}_o, \vec{z}_o)$. The axis $(O, \vec{z}_o)$ is the upward vertical.

We call $R(G, \vec{x}, \vec{y}, \vec{z})$ the direct orthonormal reference frame linked to the solid S.

A and C are its principal moments of inertia at G (C moment of inertia with respect to the axis $(G, \vec{z})$ axis of revolution symmetry of S). We assume throughout the problem that the solid S remains in contact with the horizontal plane $(O, x_o, y_o)$ at point I. The vector $\overrightarrow{IG}$ is carried by the axis $(G, \vec{w})$. The solid S is located by the usual Euler angles $\psi$, $\theta$ and $\phi$. It is in motion without slipping.

Express all results in the basis $(\vec{u}, \vec{w}, \vec{z})$.

1) Determine the instantaneous rotation velocity $\vec{\Omega}(S/R_o)$.

2) Determine the reduction elements at G of the kinetic torsor.

3) Determine the reduction elements at G of the dynamic torsor.

4) Calculate the kinetic energy of the solid with respect to $R_0$.

\textbf{Indication:}

The condition of rolling without slipping gives:
$$\vec{V}_g(D/R_o) = \vec{V}(I \in D/R_o) = \vec{V}(G/R_o) + \vec{\Omega}(D/R_o) \times \overrightarrow{GI} = \vec{0}$$
