    % --- Question e) ---
    \item À partir de la relation théorique $T^2 = \frac{4\pi^2}{C} m r^2 + \frac{4\pi^2 I_{GZ}}{C}$, la pente est $p = \frac{4\pi^2 m}{C}$.
    
    Convertissons la pente en unités SI ($\text{s}^2/\text{m}^2$) en multipliant par $10000$ :
    \[ p_{SI} = p \times 10000 = 320 \, \text{s}^2/\text{m}^2 \]
    \[\Delta p_{SI} = \Delta p \times 10000 = 20 \, \text{s}^2/\text{m}^2 \] 

    \begin{align*}
        m & = \frac{p_{SI} \cdot C}{4\pi^2} \\
        \textbf{AN: } & = \frac{320 \times 0.048}{4\pi^2} \\
        & = 0.389 \, \text{kg} \\
        \Delta m & = \frac{\Delta p_{SI} \cdot \Delta C}{4\pi^2} \\
        \textbf{AN: } & = \frac{20 \times 0.004}{4\pi^2} \\
        & = 0.002 \, \text{kg}
    \end{align*}

    Alors : $$m = 0.389 \pm 0.002 \, \text{kg}$$
