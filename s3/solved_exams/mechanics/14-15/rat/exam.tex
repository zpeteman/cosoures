\section{}
Consider a perfect rigid body \( S \) composed of a homogeneous disk \( S_1 \) of center \( D \), radius \( a \), and mass \( M \), and a homogeneous rod \( S_2 \) of center of mass \( T \), length \( L \), and mass \( m \), welded to the disk at point \( D \).

The system is in motion in the vertical plane \( X_0 OY_0 \) of the reference frame \( R_0 (O, X_0, Y_0, Z_0) \), with basis \( (\bar{x}_0, \bar{y}_0, \bar{z}_0) \), assumed to be fixed and Galilean. The disk \( S_1 \) rolls without slipping on the axis \( OY_0 \).  
\( I \) denotes the geometric contact point of \( S_1 \) with the axis \( OY_0 \), and \( y \) represents the coordinate of point \( D \) on the axis \( OY_0 \). The position of the rod relative to the vertical is defined by the angle \( \theta \). We denote by \( \bar{R} \) the reaction force exerted on the system at point \( I \).

\begin{enumerate}
    \item 
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item State the kinetic energy theorem.
        \item Prove this theorem.
    \end{enumerate}
    
    \item Calculate the inertia matrix at point \( D \) of the disk \( S_1 \).
    
    \item Calculate the inertia matrix at point \( T \) of the rod \( S_2 \) and deduce its inertia matrix at point \( D \) by applying Huygens' theorem.
    
    \item Calculate the sliding velocity of the solid and deduce the condition for rolling without slipping.
    
    \item Determine, as a function of the angle \( \theta \), the absolute velocity of point \( T \), expressed in the basis of \( R_0 \).
    
    \item Calculate the kinetic energy of the system \( E_c (S/R_0) \) as a function of \( \theta \) and \( \dot{\theta} \).
    
    \item 
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item By applying the kinetic energy theorem to the system in \( R_0 \), determine the first integral of energy.
        \item Deduce the differential equation of motion of the system.
    \end{enumerate}
    
    \item Limiting to the case of small angles and keeping only first-order terms in \( \theta \) and \( \dot{\theta} \), specify the nature of the motion and calculate the period of small oscillations \( T_0 \).
    
    \item Retrieve the differential equation of motion of the system by applying the angular momentum theorem at the geometric point \( I \) in the reference frame \( R_0 \).
\end{enumerate}
